## Regions, Taxes and Shipping
A region in the Fufuni e-commerce framework serves as a container that groups together several key entities: countries (region_countries), warehouses (region_warehouses), and shipping rates (region_shipping_rates). This grouping is fundamental to how the system handles taxes and shipping calculations, ensuring that the correct rules are applied based on the region's settings.

### Region Entities
The following entities are associated with a region:
- **Countries**: Each region can include multiple countries. The association between a region and countries is defined through the `region_countries` relation.
- **Warehouses**: Warehouses are linked to regions via `region_warehouses`, indicating which warehouses are available for shipping within that region.
- **Shipping Rates**: Shipping rates applicable to a region are specified through `region_shipping_rates`, allowing for region-specific shipping cost calculations.

## Tax Inclusive Behavior
The `tax_inclusive` flag on a region determines how taxes are displayed and calculated for items within that region. If `tax_inclusive` is set to `true`, it means that the displayed prices of items already include Value-Added Tax (VAT). The `lib/tax.ts` module handles this behavior by adapting its tax calculations accordingly. 

When `tax_inclusive` is `true`:
- The module assumes that all prices passed to it for calculation already include VAT.
- It adjusts its calculations to reflect this, ensuring that VAT is not added again.

Conversely, if `tax_inclusive` is `false`, the module calculates VAT separately and adds it to the base price of items, reflecting the total cost to the customer.
