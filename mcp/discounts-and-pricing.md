## Difference between Direct Variant Price and Regional Prices
In a multi-region production context, it's essential to differentiate between direct variant prices and regional prices. Direct variant prices are stored in the `variants` table, but these prices should not be used directly in a multi-region setup. Instead, prices by region are stored in the `variant_prices` and `shipping_rate_prices` tables. This allows for region-specific pricing, ensuring that customers are charged the correct price based on their location.

```typescript

// Correct usage: Retrieve regional price from variant_prices or shipping_rate_prices

```
