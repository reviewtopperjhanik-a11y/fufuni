## Overview of Inventory and Warehouses
The Fufuni e-commerce framework implements a comprehensive inventory management system, allowing for the tracking of stock across multiple warehouses. This system is crucial for ensuring accurate stock levels, preventing overselling, and streamlining order fulfillment.

### Stock Tracking Across Multiple Warehouses
In Fufuni, the `warehouse_inventory` table serves as the central repository for tracking stock levels. Each row in this table represents a specific stock-keeping unit (SKU) in a particular warehouse. The table contains columns for `on_hand` (the current stock level) and `reserved` (the quantity reserved for pending orders). The `warehouse_inventory_logs` table provides a historical record of all stock movements, including the reason for each movement.

To retrieve the total available quantity for a SKU, the system first checks the `warehouse_inventory` table. If the SKU is found, the system calculates the total available quantity by subtracting the reserved quantity from the on-hand quantity. If the SKU is not found in the `warehouse_inventory` table, the system falls back to the legacy `inventory` table.

### Stock Reservation and Definitive Deduction
Stock reservation occurs when a customer adds an item to their cart or places an order. During this process, the system temporarily reserves the required quantity of the SKU to prevent it from being sold to another customer. This reservation is reflected in the `reserved` column of the `warehouse_inventory` table.

Definitive deduction, on the other hand, happens when the order is finalized, and the payment is processed through Stripe. At this point, the system deducts the sold quantity from the `on_hand` column of the `warehouse_inventory` table, ensuring that the stock level is updated accurately.
